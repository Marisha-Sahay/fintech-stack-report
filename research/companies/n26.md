# N26

**Category:** neobank
**HQ / founded:** Berlin (n26-results-2026). Founded 2013; own German banking licence in 2016 (n26-mambu-case-study-2026; n26-mambu-rbi-2017).
**Scale signal:** 5.6m revenue-relevant customers, customer deposits above €10.5bn, €170.7bn annual transaction volume in 2025 (n26-results-2026).
**Last researched:** 2026-11-11

## Summary
N26 is the buy case in this cluster. After getting its banking licence in July 2016 it went live on Mambu's SaaS core banking platform in October 2016, and its then CTO said "Mambu allowed us to cut our core operating costs drastically by minimising our IT resource requirements" (n26-mambu-rbi-2017; n26-mambu-case-study-2026). Around that vendor core, N26 builds its own services: about 230 microservices in 2022, new JVM services "bootstrapped with Kotlin", over 100 PostgreSQL databases after migrating away from MySQL, Kafka added alongside AWS SQS and Kinesis, and Kubernetes on AWS replacing Nomad (n26-tech-stack-2022). A 2026 job listing says the Bank Core team runs "accounts, ledgers, and the core banking logic every other team builds on" and is "revamping that core right now", but no source read says whether Mambu is still the core or what replaces it (n26-core-systems-job-2026). No public source found describes N26's ledger model, persistence library or reconciliation design.

## Languages and frameworks
- 2018: Java and Kotlin, Spring Boot; Kotlin trialled during internal "Getting Stuff Done Days" (n26-bank-in-cloud-2018, 2018, older than 5 years).
- 2020: backend services use only Java and Kotlin, Kotlin for most; Spring Boot. Kotlin adopted "because of its simplicity and all that it brings to the table as a modern language." (Folger Fonseca, quoted in n26-hypergrowth-2020, 2020, older than 5 years).
- 2022: "A few years ago, we made a bet to invest in Kotlin and evolve the majority of our Java-based microservices." Now "all our JVM services are bootstrapped with Kotlin from the start" and "most of our Java codebase is in maintenance mode only" (n26-tech-stack-2022).
- 2026: "Our services are written almost exclusively in Kotlin on Spring Boot, deployed on Kubernetes, communicating over Kafka, backed by Postgres, and running on AWS." (n26-core-systems-job-2026).

## The money layer (ledger, balances, payments)
- N26 moved from a partner bank's backend to Mambu's cloud core banking platform once it held its own licence (n26-mambu-case-study-2026). It went live on Mambu in October 2016; a loan product launched on Mambu in February 2017, and Mambu also handles current account and overdraft features (n26-mambu-rbi-2017).
- 2026: "Bank Core runs the systems at the heart of N26 - accounts, ledgers, and the core banking logic every other team builds on." "We're revamping that core right now" (n26-core-systems-job-2026).
- Double-entry, immutability, where balances live (Mambu or N26 services), and how N26's ledgers relate to Mambu: No public source found.

## Service-layer persistence and databases
- 2020: storage is mostly SQL, with some document-based storage (n26-hypergrowth-2020).
- 2022: "Our DBA team currently helps us manage over 100 PostgreSql databases." "In the last few years, we also migrated away from Mysql, making PostgreSql our go-to RDBMS for most use cases", chosen "due to its robustness and versatility" (n26-tech-stack-2022).
- A small DBA team is possible because the database landscape is standardised and run as infrastructure as code; a new database instance can be created in a single pull request (n26-tech-stack-2022).
- ORM vs SQL-first: No public source found. The 2022 stack post does not mention Hibernate, JPA, Exposed or jOOQ (n26-tech-stack-2022).
- Messaging: "To address that, we introduced Apache Kafka to our stack", alongside existing AWS SQS and Kinesis, citing "Its capabilities, availability, resilience, and speed". Moving one flow from synchronous HTTP to async messaging cut p95 latency of a critical endpoint by 50% (n26-tech-stack-2022).

## Scale
- 2025: 5.6m revenue-relevant customers (+16%), deposits above €10.5bn, €170.7bn transaction volume (+14%), first full year of net profit (n26-results-2026).
- Services: 60+ microservices and about 15 teams (2018, n26-bank-in-cloud-2018); over 180 microservices deployed about 300 times a week (2020, n26-hypergrowth-2020); "roughly 230 microservices", over 9,000 containers, over 3TB of network data a day (2022, n26-tech-stack-2022).
- Engineering grew from about 40 to 50 people to about 300 during hypergrowth (n26-hypergrowth-2020).

## Build vs buy
- Core banking was bought, not built: Mambu SaaS from 2016 (n26-mambu-case-study-2026; n26-mambu-rbi-2017).
- Stated reasons, from N26's then CTO Gino Cordt: "Mambu allowed us to cut our core operating costs drastically by minimising our IT resource requirements" and "Mambu's ease of integration, flexibility and time to market has helped us" (n26-mambu-rbi-2017, Weak, 2017, older than 5 years). Mambu's own page says N26 needed "a flexible, scalable, and cost-effective core banking solution" (n26-mambu-case-study-2026).
- N26 still runs some "third-party software that's built with 20-year-old technology" (2022); the post does not name it (n26-tech-stack-2022).
- Everything around the core (customer-facing services, payments flows, infrastructure) is built in-house on AWS (n26-tech-stack-2022).
- Whether N26 is replacing Mambu with its own core: not confirmed. The 2026 listing says only that the core is being revamped (n26-core-systems-job-2026).

## Correctness (idempotency, reconciliation, audit)
- 2018: "everything we do needs to be audited and pass through multiple gates of security"; deployments need passing tests, no security flaws and business approval (n26-bank-in-cloud-2018).
- Idempotency and reconciliation design: No public source found.

## Changes over time (migrations and reasons)
- 2016: partner bank backend to own licence plus Mambu core (n26-mambu-case-study-2026; n26-mambu-rbi-2017).
- 2018 onward: monolith split into microservices "that can scale independently" (n26-bank-in-cloud-2018). By 2022 the monolith "was deprecated two years ago", though some early "macroservices" remain (n26-tech-stack-2022).
- After an unrestricted start, an internal tech radar limited the stack, because "it was hard to move engineers freely between teams" (n26-hypergrowth-2020).
- Java to Kotlin (2018 to 2022) (n26-bank-in-cloud-2018; n26-tech-stack-2022).
- MySQL to PostgreSQL ("robustness and versatility") (n26-tech-stack-2022).
- Nomad plus a home-built orchestration layer on SpotInst to Kubernetes, because "the pipelines running with the homegrown solution became increasingly slower"; deployments went from about 1.5 hours to about 30 minutes (n26-tech-stack-2022).
- Kafka added next to SQS and Kinesis; Jenkins to GitHub Actions and Argo CD; ELK to OpenSearch (n26-tech-stack-2022).
